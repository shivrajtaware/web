Product Overview

Built for the Way a Hospital Actually Runs.

  • —Connects clinical and administrative teams
  • —Brings patient records into one place
  • —Structures billing and financial workflows
  • —Supports pharmacy and inventory tracking
  • —Gives departments real-time visibility

The Estoniz Hospital Management System was developed to bring the many moving parts of a hospital — patients, doctors, nurses, pharmacy, laboratory, billing, and administration — into a single, structured digital environment.

Rather than treating a hospital as a single generic workflow, HMS is organized around how different departments actually operate day to day, while still keeping data connected across the entire facility.

The result is a platform where a patient's journey — from registration to discharge — is tracked consistently, and where staff across departments work from the same accurate information instead of disconnected paper records or isolated spreadsheets.

FAQ

Common Questions About HMS

What is the Estoniz Hospital Management System?

HMS is a comprehensive hospital management platform developed by Estoniz that connects clinical, administrative, financial, and operational workflows in one digital environment.

Is HMS currently deployed at a hospital?

Yes. HMS is currently operational at a hospital, supporting real day-to-day clinical and administrative workflows.

What modules does HMS include?

HMS covers patient management, appointments, telemedicine, EMR, doctor workflows, nursing, pharmacy, inventory, laboratory, radiology, billing, emergency, bed management, ICU, OT, and reporting and analytics.

Can HMS be customized for a specific hospital?

Estoniz builds and adapts HMS around the operational workflows of the organization it's implemented for, rather than applying a fixed, one-size-fits-all setup.

How do I book a demo?

Use the demo request form above with your hospital's details, and the Estoniz team will follow up to arrange a walkthrough.

Who develops and supports HMS?

HMS is developed and supported by Estoniz, a software solutions company focused on practical, real-world digital systems.
